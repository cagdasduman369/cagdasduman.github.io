City View

Philadelphia · Pennsylvania · United States

Philadelphia is a city where America's past and present meet in a remarkable urban landscape. As one of the nation's oldest major cities, it combines historic buildings, elegant architectural landmarks, distinctive neighborhoods, and a modern skyline. From the streets of Center City to panoramic views across the urban landscape, Philadelphia reveals a character shaped by centuries of history and continuous transformation. These photographs capture my impressions of Philadelphia's cityscape, its architectural contrasts, and the atmosphere of a city that has played an important role in American history. Each view offers a different perspective on its streets, buildings, and distinctive urban identity.
