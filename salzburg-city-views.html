City Views, Salzburg

Salzburg, Austria

Salzburg is a city where historic architecture and natural beauty come together. Set along the Salzach River and surrounded by the Austrian Alps, its skyline features elegant church towers, historic rooftops, and the impressive Hohensalzburg Fortress. The city's UNESCO-listed historic center is filled with charming streets, beautiful squares, and remarkable architectural details. Beyond its famous landmarks, Salzburg offers scenic viewpoints and peaceful riverside settings that reveal its unique character. This collection brings together my personal photographs and impressions of the city's architecture, landscapes, and everyday atmosphere.
