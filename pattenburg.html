Pattenburg

New Jersey · United States

Pattenburg is a small community in Hunterdon County, New Jersey, surrounded by the peaceful landscapes and rural charm of the American countryside. Far from the busy atmosphere of major cities, it offers a glimpse into a quieter and more traditional side of New Jersey. Its open spaces, scenic surroundings, and local character reflect the beauty of rural life in this part of the state. These photographs capture my personal experiences and memories of Pattenburg and its distinctive atmosphere.
