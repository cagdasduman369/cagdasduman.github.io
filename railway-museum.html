Railway Museum

Baku · Azerbaijan

Baku's railway heritage reflects an important part of Azerbaijan's industrial and transportation history. The city's railway development played a significant role in connecting the capital with other regions and supporting the growth of trade and industry. The Railway Museum offers an opportunity to explore this history through railway-related exhibits, historical photographs and objects connected with the development of the railway network. These displays provide a glimpse into the technological and social changes that shaped transportation in Azerbaijan. During my visit, I photographed the exhibits and details that caught my attention, documenting this part of Baku's historical heritage. This collection brings together my personal photographs and memories from the Railway Museum.
