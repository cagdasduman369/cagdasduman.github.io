Railway Museum

Baku · Azerbaijan

Azerbaijan's railway history is closely connected to the country's industrial development, economic growth, and regional connections. As an important transportation hub, Baku played a major role in the expansion of railway networks linking the capital with other parts of the country and neighboring regions. The Railway Museum presents this heritage through historical photographs, railway equipment, documents, and objects from different periods. Its exhibits illustrate how railway transportation evolved over time and contributed to the movement of people, goods, and ideas, becoming an important part of Azerbaijan's modern history.

Visiting the museum offered an opportunity to discover a different dimension of Baku beyond its familiar architectural landmarks and waterfront attractions. I explored the exhibition and photographed the historical objects, displays, and details that reflect the development of railway transportation. The collection also highlights the technological changes and historical memories associated with this important part of the country's heritage. These photographs preserve my impressions of the museum and document my visit to one of Baku's distinctive cultural sites.
