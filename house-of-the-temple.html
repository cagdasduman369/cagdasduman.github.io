House of the Temple

Washington, DC · United States

The House of the Temple is one of the most distinctive architectural landmarks in Washington, DC, and serves as the headquarters of the Scottish Rite of Freemasonry, Southern Jurisdiction. Completed in 1915, the building was designed by architect John Russell Pope and inspired by the ancient Mausoleum at Halicarnassus, one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World. Its monumental structure, impressive columns, and carefully balanced proportions give it a remarkable presence among the city's historic buildings. The interior also features grand spaces, decorative details, and a library containing an extensive collection of books and historical materials. Beyond its architectural significance, the building reflects the history and traditions of Freemasonry in the United States. Its unusual design makes it a fascinating stop for visitors interested in architecture, history, and cultural heritage.
