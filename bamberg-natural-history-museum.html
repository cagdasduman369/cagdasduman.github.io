Bamberg Natural History Museum

Bamberg, Germany · Bavaria

The Natural History Museum in Bamberg offers a fascinating opportunity to explore the diversity of the natural world and discover the scientific stories behind its collections. Housed in a historic building, the museum brings together specimens from different areas of natural science, offering insights into wildlife, geology, and the remarkable variety of life on Earth. One of its most distinctive spaces is the historic Bird Hall (Vogelsaal), where carefully arranged bird specimens create an impressive exhibition that reflects the museum's long tradition of scientific observation and natural history research.
