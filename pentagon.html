The Pentagon Memorial

Arlington · Virginia · United States

The Pentagon, located in Arlington, Virginia, is the headquarters of the United States Department of Defense. Completed in 1943 during World War II, this enormous five-sided building is one of the most recognizable government buildings in the world and a major symbol of the United States military establishment.

Beyond its architectural design and institutional importance, the Pentagon is also a place of remembrance. The Pentagon Memorial, located nearby, honors the victims of the September 11, 2001 attacks. The site offers visitors an opportunity to reflect on an important and deeply significant chapter in American history.
