Maksimir Park

Zagreb · Croatia
Maksimir Park is one of Zagreb's most beloved green spaces and a peaceful escape from the busy rhythm of the city. Established in the late 18th century, it is among the oldest public parks in Southeast Europe. Its extensive woodland paths, open meadows, lakes and landscaped gardens create a beautiful setting for walking, relaxing and enjoying nature. The park offers a different perspective on Zagreb, revealing the city's appreciation for green spaces and outdoor life. These photographs capture my personal memories of this tranquil corner of the Croatian capital.
