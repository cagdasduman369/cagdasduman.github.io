Cityscape & Views

Baku · Azerbaijan

Baku offers a striking combination of historic architecture, modern skyscrapers and beautiful views along the Caspian Sea. The city's skyline reveals different sides of its character, from traditional neighborhoods and historic landmarks to contemporary buildings and impressive waterfront scenery.

The Flame Towers have become one of the city's most recognizable landmarks, creating a distinctive silhouette against the skyline, especially after sunset. Along the waterfront, Baku Boulevard offers beautiful views of the Caspian Sea and the city's modern architecture.

The contrast between the old and new parts of Baku makes exploring the city particularly interesting. From wide panoramic viewpoints to lively streets and impressive buildings, every corner offers a different perspective on the Azerbaijani capital.
