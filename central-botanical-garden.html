Central Botanical Garden

Baku · Azerbaijan

Away from the busy streets of Baku, Central Botanical Garden offers a different perspective on the city. Its green spaces, winding paths and variety of trees and plants create a calm setting for a leisurely walk. The garden brings together different types of vegetation, from mature trees and leafy corners to carefully maintained plant areas. Depending on the season, its scenery changes with different shades of green, flowering plants and changing light. It is a pleasant place to slow down, enjoy the natural surroundings and take a break from the rhythm of the capital. I enjoyed exploring its paths, noticing the small details of the landscape and discovering this quieter side of Baku. The contrast between the garden's natural atmosphere and the surrounding urban environment makes it an interesting place to visit. These photographs bring together my impressions, favourite views and personal memories from my time in Central Botanical Garden.
