Central Botanical Garden

Baku · Azerbaijan

Away from the busy streets of Baku, Central Botanical Garden offers a different perspective on the city. Its green spaces, winding paths and variety of trees and plants create a calm setting for a leisurely walk. The garden is a pleasant place to slow down, enjoy the natural surroundings and take a break from the rhythm of the capital. I enjoyed discovering this quieter side of Baku and capturing the details that make the garden feel distinct from the city's urban landscape. These photographs reflect my visit and the moments I experienced while exploring the garden.
