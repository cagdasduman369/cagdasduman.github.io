Batumi Boulevard

Batumi · Georgia

Stretching along the Black Sea coastline, Batumi Boulevard is one of the city's most iconic and lively places. Its long waterfront promenade combines palm-lined pathways, green spaces, modern sculptures, cafés, and beautiful sea views. It is a popular destination for walking, cycling, relaxing by the water, and enjoying Batumi's vibrant seaside atmosphere. The boulevard offers a wonderful perspective of the city, especially during sunset and in the evening. These photographs capture my personal memories of Batumi Boulevard and its coastal charm.
