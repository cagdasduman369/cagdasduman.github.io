Pecka Climbing Area

Pecka · Mrkonjić Grad · Bosnia and Herzegovina

Located near the village of Pecka in western Bosnia and Herzegovina, this climbing area is surrounded by the impressive limestone landscapes and natural beauty of the region. Its rocky formations offer an opportunity to experience outdoor climbing in a peaceful rural setting, far from the crowds of larger tourist destinations. Pecka has become a meeting point for climbing enthusiasts and nature lovers, combining outdoor adventure with the welcoming atmosphere of the local community.
