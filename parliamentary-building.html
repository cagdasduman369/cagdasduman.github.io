Parliament Building

Budapest · Hungary

The Hungarian Parliament Building is one of Budapest's most recognizable landmarks and among the finest examples of Gothic Revival architecture in Europe. Standing majestically on the eastern bank of the Danube, its elaborate façade, towering dome, and symmetrical design create an impressive presence along the riverfront.

Completed in the early 20th century, the building reflects Hungary's national identity and architectural ambitions. Its riverside setting makes it especially striking, with the surrounding bridges and historic cityscape providing a remarkable backdrop.
