Tirana, Albania

Albania · Capital City

Tirana, the capital of Albania, is a city shaped by centuries of history and cultural change. Its architecture brings together Ottoman-era landmarks, Italian-influenced buildings, and reminders of the communist period, while colorful streets, lively squares, and cafés give the city its distinctive character. Today, Tirana combines its historical heritage with the energy of a modern Balkan capital. Its museums, landmarks, and diverse neighborhoods offer different perspectives on Albania's past and present. These photographs capture my personal impressions of the city and its everyday atmosphere.
