İçəri Şəhər (Old City)

Baku · Azerbaijan

At the heart of Baku lies İçəri Şəhər, the historic Old City, where centuries of Azerbaijani history are reflected in its narrow streets, stone walls and traditional architecture. Walking through its old neighbourhoods feels like stepping into a different period of the city's past. Small courtyards, historic buildings, traditional shops and quiet alleyways give this part of Baku a character that is quite different from the modern city surrounding it. The Old City is home to some of Baku's most recognisable landmarks, including the Maiden Tower and the Palace of the Shirvanshahs. Yet its atmosphere is not defined by monuments alone. Everyday life continues among the historic streets, where visitors can discover local cafés, handicraft shops and architectural details that are easy to miss when walking too quickly.

During my visit, I explored these streets and photographed the details that caught my attention, from old stone façades and traditional doorways to the changing views around the historic neighbourhood. This collection brings together my personal photographs and memories of İçəri Şəhər, one of the most distinctive places in Baku.
