Craiova – City View

Romania · Oltenia

Craiova, one of the most distinctive cities in southwestern Romania, reveals its character through a fascinating combination of historic architecture, elegant public spaces and everyday urban life. As the cultural heart of the Oltenia region, the city offers a welcoming atmosphere shaped by its rich history and evolving contemporary identity. Its streets and squares bring together beautifully preserved buildings, local cafés, green spaces and lively meeting places. Walking through Craiova is an opportunity to appreciate the details of its architecture, discover the rhythm of its neighbourhoods and experience a city where Romanian traditions and modern life exist side by side.
