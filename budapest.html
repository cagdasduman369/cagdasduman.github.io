Budapest

Hungary · Central Europe

Budapest is a city that reveals its character through contrasts. Along the Danube, monumental landmarks stand beside everyday urban life, while the historic streets of Buda give way to the grand boulevards and lively neighbourhoods of Pest. The city's architecture reflects centuries of Hungarian history, from medieval heritage and imperial grandeur to modern cultural influences. Its bridges, riverside promenades, thermal baths, and expansive parks each offer a different way to experience the capital. Whether viewed from the heights around Buda Castle or explored at street level, Budapest has a distinctive atmosphere that rewards curiosity.
