Budapest

Hungary · Central Europe

Budapest, the capital of Hungary, is a city shaped by history, architecture, and the Danube River. Its historic landmarks, green spaces, impressive city views, and riverside scenery create a distinctive atmosphere on both sides of the river.

During my time in Budapest, I explored different parts of the city and discovered its architectural highlights, public spaces, and memorable viewpoints. Each place offered a different perspective on the Hungarian capital, making Budapest a memorable stop in my travels through Central Europe.
