Gomi Mountain

Ozurgeti · Guria · Georgia

Gomi Mountain, located in Georgia's Guria region, is known for its breathtaking mountain scenery and spectacular views above the clouds. At certain times of the year, the mountain landscape creates the impression of floating above a sea of clouds, offering an unforgettable natural spectacle. Its peaceful atmosphere, fresh mountain air, and panoramic landscapes make it a special destination for nature lovers and travelers exploring western Georgia. These photographs capture my personal memories of Gomi Mountain and its remarkable scenery.
