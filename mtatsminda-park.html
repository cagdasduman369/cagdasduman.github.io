Mtatsminda Park

Tbilisi · Georgia

Located on Mount Mtatsminda overlooking the Georgian capital, Mtatsminda Park is one of Tbilisi's best-known recreational destinations. The park offers panoramic views of the city, green spaces, walking areas, and various attractions. Its elevated location makes it a wonderful place to enjoy Tbilisi from above and experience a different perspective of the city.
