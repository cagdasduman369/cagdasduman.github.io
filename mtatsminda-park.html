Mtatsminda Park

Tbilisi · Georgia

Located on Mount Mtatsminda overlooking the Georgian capital, Mtatsminda Park is one of Tbilisi's best-known recreational destinations. The park offers panoramic views of the city, green spaces, walking areas, and various attractions. Its elevated location makes it a wonderful place to enjoy Tbilisi from above and experience a different perspective of the city. Visitors can reach the area by the historic funicular railway, making the journey itself part of the experience. The park combines open spaces and entertainment with beautiful scenery, offering something for visitors of different ages. From its viewpoints, the rooftops, landmarks, and surrounding hills create an impressive panorama of the Georgian capital. It is also a popular place for families and locals to spend time away from the busy streets below.
