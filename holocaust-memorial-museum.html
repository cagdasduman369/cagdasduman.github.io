United States Holocaust Memorial Museum

Washington, DC · United States

The United States Holocaust Memorial Museum in Washington, DC, is a national institution dedicated to documenting, studying, and remembering the Holocaust. Opened in 1993, the museum presents the history of the systematic persecution and murder of six million European Jews by Nazi Germany and its collaborators, while also examining the experiences of other groups targeted by the regime. Through historical objects, photographs, documents, personal belongings, and survivor testimonies, its exhibitions offer visitors a deeply moving account of this period. The museum's carefully designed spaces guide visitors through the events that led to the Holocaust, the realities of life in Nazi-occupied Europe, and the aftermath of the genocide. Beyond preserving historical evidence, the institution seeks to encourage reflection on human dignity, responsibility, and the dangers of hatred and intolerance. A visit is a powerful reminder of the importance of historical memory and education in preventing future atrocities.
