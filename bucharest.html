Bucharest, Romania

Romania · Wallachia

Bucharest, the capital of Romania, is a city where layers of history, architectural contrasts and contemporary urban life come together. Its broad boulevards, elegant historic buildings and distinctive Belle Époque architecture reveal traces of a past that once earned it the nickname "Little Paris." At the same time, imposing communist-era structures, lively streets and modern cultural spaces reflect the city's complex transformation. Walking through Bucharest offers an opportunity to discover a capital shaped by different eras, where historic character and present-day energy coexist in a fascinating urban landscape.
