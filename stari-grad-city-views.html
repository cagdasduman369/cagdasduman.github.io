Stari Grad · Hvar Island · Croatia
Stari Grad reveals its character through its peaceful streets, traditional stone houses and beautiful views across the historic town. Walking through its narrow lanes, visitors can discover charming rooftops, old architecture and glimpses of the Adriatic landscape. These photographs capture the atmosphere of the town and the everyday beauty of one of the oldest settlements on the Croatian coast.
