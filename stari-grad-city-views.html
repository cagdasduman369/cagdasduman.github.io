City Views

Stari Grad · Hvar Island · Croatia

Stari Grad reveals its character through its peaceful streets, traditional stone houses and beautiful views across the historic town. Walking through its narrow lanes, visitors can discover charming rooftops, old architecture and glimpses of the Adriatic landscape. The town's historic center preserves the atmosphere of a traditional Dalmatian settlement, where centuries-old buildings and quiet courtyards form an important part of everyday life. Its coastal setting adds another dimension to the scenery, with beautiful perspectives across the surrounding landscape and the nearby sea. These photographs capture the atmosphere of the town and the everyday beauty of one of the oldest settlements on the Croatian coast.
