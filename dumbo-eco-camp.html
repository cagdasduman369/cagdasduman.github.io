Dumbo Eco Camp

Ozurgeti · Guria · Georgia

Dumbo Eco Camp offers a peaceful escape surrounded by the beautiful rural landscapes of Georgia's Guria region. Away from the busy city atmosphere, this place provides an opportunity to enjoy nature, relax, and experience the slower rhythm of countryside life. Its natural surroundings and welcoming atmosphere make it a memorable stop for travelers exploring western Georgia.
