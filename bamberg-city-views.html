Bamberg City Views

Bamberg, Germany · Bavaria

Bamberg is a historic town in northern Bavaria, Germany, known for its remarkably preserved medieval architecture, picturesque Old Town, charming half-timbered houses, and scenic views along the Regnitz River. Its unique blend of history, architecture, and riverside landscapes makes Bamberg one of Germany's most beautiful historic towns.
