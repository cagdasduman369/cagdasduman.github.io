6 May Park

Batumi · Georgia

Located in the heart of Batumi, 6 May Park is one of the city's oldest and most beloved green spaces. With its peaceful pathways, beautiful trees, and relaxing atmosphere, the park offers a pleasant escape from the busy streets of the city. It is a popular place for walking, spending time outdoors, and enjoying nature in the middle of Batumi. These photographs capture my personal memories of 6 May Park and its pleasant atmosphere. The park's green surroundings provide a refreshing contrast to the city's lively urban landscape. It is also a lovely place to slow down and experience a quieter side of Batumi.
