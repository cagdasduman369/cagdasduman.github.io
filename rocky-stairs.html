Rocky Stairs

Philadelphia · Pennsylvania · United States

The Rocky Steps, located at the Philadelphia Museum of Art, are among the city's most recognizable landmarks. Made famous by the Rocky film series, these iconic steps have become a popular destination for visitors from around the world.

Climbing the steps offers a memorable experience and a wonderful view of the Benjamin Franklin Parkway and the surrounding city. This collection captures my personal photographs and memories from this famous Philadelphia landmark.
