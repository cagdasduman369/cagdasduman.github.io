Rocky Stairs

Philadelphia · Pennsylvania · United States

The Rocky Steps, located at the Philadelphia Museum of Art, are among the city's most recognizable landmarks. Made famous by the Rocky film series, these iconic steps have become a popular destination for visitors from around the world and an enduring symbol of determination and achievement. Climbing the 72 steps offers a memorable experience, especially for fans of the films who enjoy recreating the famous scene featuring Rocky Balboa. At the top, visitors are rewarded with a wonderful view of the Benjamin Franklin Parkway and the surrounding city. The nearby museum adds further cultural significance to this popular destination, bringing together Philadelphia's artistic heritage and cinematic history. This collection captures my personal photographs and memories from this famous Philadelphia landmark, including its lively atmosphere and the impressive views from the top.
