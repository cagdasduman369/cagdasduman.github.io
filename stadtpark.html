Stadtpark

Austria · Vienna · Stadtpark

Stadtpark is one of Vienna's best-known public parks, offering green lawns, tree-lined paths, and peaceful places to relax in the middle of the city. The park is also famous for its monuments and sculptures, including the golden statue of composer Johann Strauss.
