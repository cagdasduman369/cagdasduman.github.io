Times Square

New York City · United States

Times Square is one of the most iconic and energetic destinations in New York City, situated at the heart of Manhattan. Famous for its spectacular digital billboards, dazzling lights, Broadway theatres, and constant flow of people, this world-famous intersection captures the rhythm and diversity of New York. Surrounded by entertainment venues, restaurants, shops, and towering buildings, Times Square offers a remarkable experience that reflects the city's vibrant urban character. Whether during the day or illuminated after dark, its atmosphere is unlike anywhere else in the world. These photographs preserve my personal memories of exploring one of the most recognizable landmarks in New York City.
