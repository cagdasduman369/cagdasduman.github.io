New York City · United States

New York City is famous for its impressive skyline, distinctive architecture and breathtaking urban landscapes. From the towering skyscrapers of Manhattan to the views across the East River, the city offers countless opportunities to admire its remarkable urban scenery. Its streets, waterfronts and elevated viewpoints reveal different perspectives of a city that is constantly moving and evolving.
