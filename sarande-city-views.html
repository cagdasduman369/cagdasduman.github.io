Sarandë City Views

Sarandë · Albanian Riviera · Albania

Sarandë's cityscape unfolds along one of the most striking bays of the Albanian Riviera, where buildings climb the surrounding hills and the Ionian Sea provides a vivid blue backdrop. From its waterfront promenade to its hillside streets, the town reveals a fascinating combination of Mediterranean scenery, contemporary development, and the everyday life of a growing coastal destination. Its changing skyline reflects Sarandë's transformation into one of southern Albania's best-known seaside towns.

Exploring Sarandë on foot offers a different perspective from simply enjoying its beaches. Streets open onto unexpected sea views, balconies overlook the bay, and the light changes the appearance of the town throughout the day. These photographs capture my personal impressions of Sarandë beyond the shoreline, documenting its urban landscapes, architectural contrasts, and distinctive relationship with the Ionian coast.
