Regional Museum, Maribor

Slovenia · Maribor

The Maribor Regional Museum offers an opportunity to discover the history, culture and heritage of the city and the surrounding region. Located in the historic heart of Maribor, the museum preserves a rich collection that reflects different periods of local life and cultural development.

Visiting the museum was a chance to learn more about Maribor's past and appreciate the historical character of this Slovenian city. I enjoyed exploring its surroundings and capturing some memories of my visit.
