The Metropolitan Museum of Art

New York City · United States

The Metropolitan Museum of Art, widely known as The Met, is one of the world's most renowned art museums, located along Fifth Avenue beside Central Park in Manhattan. Founded in 1870, the museum houses an extraordinary collection spanning thousands of years and cultures, from ancient Egyptian artifacts and classical sculptures to European paintings and works of modern art. Its impressive galleries and architectural details make it an essential cultural destination in New York City. Visiting The Met offers an opportunity to explore the artistic achievements and cultural heritage of civilizations from around the world.
