The Metropolitan Museum of Art

New York City · United States

The Metropolitan Museum of Art, widely known as The Met, is one of the world's most distinguished cultural institutions, located along Fifth Avenue beside Central Park in Manhattan. Founded in 1870, the museum brings together an extraordinary collection of art and historical objects spanning more than five thousand years of human creativity. From ancient Egyptian artifacts and classical sculptures to European masterpieces, Asian art, and modern works, its galleries offer a remarkable journey through the artistic achievements of civilizations across the world. Beyond its collections, the museum's impressive architecture and carefully designed exhibition spaces make it one of New York City's most memorable cultural destinations.
