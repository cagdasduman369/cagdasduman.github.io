Gornji Milanovac, Serbia

Šumadija Region, Central Serbia

Set among the green hills of the Šumadija region, Gornji Milanovac combines a relaxed small-town atmosphere with a rich historical background. Founded in the nineteenth century, the town is closely connected to the story of modern Serbia, with the nearby village of Takovo holding particular importance in the country's history. Its surrounding landscapes and quiet streets make it a memorable place to explore and photograph.
