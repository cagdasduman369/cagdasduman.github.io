Gornji Milanovac, Serbia

Šumadija Region, Central Serbia

Surrounded by the rolling hills and green landscapes of central Serbia, Gornji Milanovac offers a glimpse into the historical heritage and everyday life of the Šumadija region. Established in 1853, the town developed during a formative period in modern Serbian history and is known for its orderly streets, welcoming town center, and connection to the surrounding countryside. Nearby, the village of Takovo holds particular historical significance as the place where Miloš Obrenović initiated the Second Serbian Uprising in 1815, an important turning point in Serbia's struggle for autonomy from Ottoman rule. The wider area combines cultural landmarks with wooded hills, rural settlements, and scenic roads that invite exploration beyond the main urban center.
