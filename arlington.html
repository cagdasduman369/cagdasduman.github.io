Arlington National Cemetery

Arlington · Virginia · United States

Arlington National Cemetery, located in Arlington, Virginia, is one of the most significant military cemeteries in the United States. Established during the American Civil War in 1864, it serves as the final resting place for thousands of American service members, veterans, and distinguished national figures. Among its most recognized landmarks are the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier, the eternal flame at President John F. Kennedy's gravesite, and the Memorial Amphitheater. The cemetery stands as a powerful symbol of military service, sacrifice, and national remembrance.

Walking through Arlington National Cemetery offers a deeply moving experience. Its endless rows of white headstones, carefully maintained grounds, and solemn atmosphere reflect generations of American history and the human cost of war. Beyond its historical importance, the cemetery is also a place for reflection, respect, and remembrance.
