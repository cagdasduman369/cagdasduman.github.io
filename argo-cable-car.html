Argo Cable Car

Batumi · Georgia

Argo Cable Car is one of Batumi's most popular attractions, offering a memorable journey from the city center to Anuria Mountain. As the cable car rises above the city, visitors can enjoy panoramic views of Batumi, the Black Sea, and the surrounding landscape. At the upper station, the observation area provides an impressive perspective of the city's skyline and coastline. These photographs capture my personal memories of the Argo Cable Car experience and the beautiful views of Batumi.
