Mleeta, South Lebanon

Lebanon · South Lebanon

Located in the hills of South Lebanon, Mleeta is a distinctive historical site surrounded by a scenic natural landscape. Its outdoor displays, preserved structures, exhibition spaces, and panoramic views offer visitors an opportunity to explore a place where local history and the surrounding environment come together.

During my visit, I had the opportunity to walk around the site, discover its different sections, and appreciate the atmosphere of this unique location. These photographs capture my personal impressions of Mleeta as part of my travels through Lebanon.
