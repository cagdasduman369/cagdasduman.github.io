Miniature Park

Shekvetili · Georgia

Miniature Park in Shekvetili offers visitors the opportunity to discover some of Georgia's most famous landmarks and architectural attractions in miniature form. Set among landscaped gardens, the park brings together detailed models of historic monuments, churches, and notable buildings from different parts of the country. Walking through the park provides an interesting overview of Georgia's cultural and architectural heritage in one place. These photographs capture my personal travel memories of Miniature Park.
