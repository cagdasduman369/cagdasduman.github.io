Miniature Park

Shekvetili · Georgia

Miniature Park in Shekvetili offers visitors the opportunity to discover some of Georgia's most famous landmarks and architectural attractions in miniature form. Set among landscaped gardens, the park brings together detailed models of historic monuments, churches, and notable buildings from different parts of the country. Walking through the park provides an interesting overview of Georgia's cultural and architectural heritage in one place. Each miniature offers a closer look at the country's distinctive architecture and historical identity, allowing visitors to appreciate places they might otherwise need to travel across Georgia to see. The carefully arranged models and surrounding green spaces create an enjoyable setting for exploring and photography. It is also an interesting way to discover the diversity of Georgia's regions, traditions, and architectural styles.
