The White House

Washington, DC · United States

The White House is the official residence and workplace of the President of the United States and one of the most recognizable landmarks in the country. Located at 1600 Pennsylvania Avenue NW in Washington, DC, it has served as the home of every American president since John Adams, who moved into the building in 1800. Beyond its role as a presidential residence, the White House is an important center of executive government, hosting official meetings, diplomatic visits, and major national events. Its neoclassical architecture, white-painted exterior, and historic rooms have become familiar symbols of the American presidency.

The surrounding grounds and nearby Lafayette Square form part of the historic landscape of the nation's capital. Over more than two centuries, the building has witnessed important moments in American political and social history, while also undergoing several renovations and expansions.
