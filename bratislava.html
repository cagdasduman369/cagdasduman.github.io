Bratislava, Slovakia

Slovakia · Bratislava Region

Bratislava, Slovakia's capital, lies on the banks of the Danube near the borders of Austria and Hungary, giving the city a distinctive position at the crossroads of Central European history and culture. Its compact Old Town is filled with historic squares, narrow streets, colourful façades, and architectural traces of different periods, while Bratislava Castle rises above the city with sweeping views of the river and surrounding landscape. Beyond the historic centre, the city combines cultural institutions, lively cafés, modern districts, and everyday urban life in a relaxed and approachable atmosphere. Nearby, the dramatic ruins of Devin Castle overlook the meeting point of the Danube and Morava rivers, revealing another layer of the region's past. These photographs preserve my personal impressions of Bratislava and its landmarks, from the city's historic heart to the places that make its wider landscape distinctive.
