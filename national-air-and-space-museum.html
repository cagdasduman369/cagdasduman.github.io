National Air and Space Museum

Washington, DC · United States

The National Air and Space Museum in Washington, DC, is one of the world's leading institutions dedicated to the history of aviation and space exploration. Part of the Smithsonian Institution, it preserves and presents an extraordinary collection of aircraft, spacecraft, scientific instruments, and historical objects that document humanity's efforts to explore the skies and beyond. Its exhibitions trace the development of flight, from early aviation pioneers to the technological achievements that made modern air travel possible.

Visitors can also discover important milestones in space exploration, including the history of human spaceflight and the scientific missions that expanded our understanding of the universe. Through original artifacts, interactive displays, and educational presentations, the museum connects scientific progress with the people and ideas behind it. Its collections offer a fascinating perspective on innovation, engineering, and the human desire to explore the unknown.
