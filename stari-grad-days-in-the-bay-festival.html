Days in the Bay Festival

Stari Grad · Hvar Island · Croatia

Days in the Bay is a lively festival that brings music, culture and entertainment to the beautiful waterfront of Stari Grad on Hvar Island. Set against the backdrop of the historic harbour and traditional stone architecture, the event adds a vibrant atmosphere to this peaceful Adriatic town. The festival offers visitors an opportunity to experience local life through performances, social gatherings and celebrations along the bay. As the streets and waterfront come alive, Stari Grad reveals a different side of its character, combining its rich heritage with contemporary cultural experiences. These photographs capture my personal memories of the festival and the special atmosphere of Stari Grad during the celebrations.
