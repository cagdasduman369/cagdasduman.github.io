Ozurgeti, Georgia

Guria · Western Georgia

Located in the Guria region of western Georgia, Ozurgeti is surrounded by beautiful landscapes, green mountains, and traditional Georgian villages. The wider area offers a chance to experience a quieter side of the country, away from its busy tourist destinations. From the peaceful atmosphere of rural guesthouses to the impressive views of Gomi Mountain, this region offers memorable opportunities to explore nature and local life. These photographs capture my personal travel memories of Ozurgeti and its surroundings. The region is also known for its welcoming atmosphere and distinctive rural character. Its natural scenery offers a glimpse into everyday life in western Georgia.
