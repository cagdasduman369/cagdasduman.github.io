Basilica of the National Shrine of the Immaculate Conception

Washington, DC · United States

Located in Washington, DC, the Basilica of the National Shrine of the Immaculate Conception is one of the largest Catholic churches in North America and one of the city's most impressive architectural landmarks. Construction began in 1920, and the basilica is distinguished by its monumental scale, Byzantine and Romanesque architectural influences, and remarkable artistic details. Its interior features elaborate mosaics, colorful decorations, and numerous chapels dedicated to different cultural and religious traditions.

The peaceful atmosphere offers visitors an opportunity to appreciate both the building's artistic beauty and its spiritual significance. During my visit, I was particularly struck by the richness of its interior and the craftsmanship visible throughout the building. These photographs capture some of the details and impressions from my visit to this remarkable landmark in the American capital.
