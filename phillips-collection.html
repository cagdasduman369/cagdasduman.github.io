The Phillips Collection

Washington, DC · United States

Founded in 1921 by art collector Duncan Phillips, The Phillips Collection is widely recognized as America's first museum of modern art. Located in the historic Dupont Circle neighborhood of Washington, DC, the museum brings together an outstanding collection of modern and contemporary works in an intimate and welcoming setting. Its galleries feature paintings by influential artists such as Pierre-Auguste Renoir, Vincent van Gogh, Paul Cézanne, Henri Matisse, and Mark Rothko. One of its best-known works is Renoir's *Luncheon of the Boating Party*, a celebrated example of Impressionist painting. The museum's combination of historic rooms and carefully arranged exhibitions creates a distinctive atmosphere that encourages visitors to experience art at a personal level. Beyond its permanent collection, it also hosts temporary exhibitions and cultural programs.
