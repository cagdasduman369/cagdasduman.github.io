Novi Sad, Serbia

Serbia · Danube River

Novi Sad is a historic city in northern Serbia, situated along the banks of the Danube River. Known for its charming architecture, cultural atmosphere, lively streets, and the impressive Petrovaradin Fortress, the city offers a unique blend of history and modern life.
