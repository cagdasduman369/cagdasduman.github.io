Library of Congress

Washington, DC · United States

The Library of Congress is the largest library in the world and one of the most important cultural and research institutions in the United States. Established in 1800, it preserves an extraordinary collection of books, manuscripts, maps, photographs, recordings, and historical documents from around the globe. Its most famous building, the Thomas Jefferson Building, is especially admired for its impressive Beaux-Arts architecture, grand staircases, colorful mosaics, detailed murals, and richly decorated interiors. Opened to the public in 1897, the building reflects the ambition to create a national institution that celebrates knowledge, learning, and artistic achievement. Visitors can explore its magnificent public spaces while discovering exhibitions that present different aspects of American and world history. The library's collections make it an invaluable resource for researchers and an important guardian of cultural heritage.
