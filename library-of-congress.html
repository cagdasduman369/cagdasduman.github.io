Library of Congress

Washington, DC · United States

The Library of Congress is the largest library in the world and one of Washington, DC's most remarkable cultural landmarks. Its historic Thomas Jefferson Building is especially admired for its grand architecture, ornate interiors, beautiful murals, and decorative details. Beyond its impressive appearance, the institution preserves an extraordinary collection of books, manuscripts, photographs, maps, and other historical materials.
