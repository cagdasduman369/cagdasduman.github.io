Devín Castle

Slovakia · Bratislava

Perched dramatically on a rocky cliff above the meeting point of the Danube and Morava rivers, Devín Castle is one of Slovakia's most remarkable historical landmarks. Its strategic position near the borders of Austria and Slovakia has made it an important place throughout centuries of Central European history. Today, the castle ruins offer a fascinating glimpse into the past, while the surrounding landscape creates an extraordinary setting for exploration. Walking through the remains of its ancient walls and towers, visitors can enjoy breathtaking views across the rivers and the countryside. Devín Castle is a place where history, nature and spectacular scenery come together.
