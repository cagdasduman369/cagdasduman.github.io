Sarandë, Albania

Albania · Albanian Riviera · Ionian Coast

Sarandë is one of Albania’s most captivating coastal towns, stretching along a sweeping bay where the turquoise waters of the Ionian Sea meet the sunlit hills of the southern Riviera. Shaped by centuries of Mediterranean exchange, the town reflects a rich history that reaches back to antiquity, while its waterfront, palm-lined promenade, and lively streets reveal the character of a modern seaside destination. Beyond its beaches, Sarandë also offers a gateway to the cultural and natural treasures of southern Albania, from ancient archaeological sites to secluded coves and dramatic coastal landscapes.

What makes Sarandë memorable is the way the sea shapes both its scenery and its rhythm of life. Fishing boats share the horizon with ferries, cafés open onto the waterfront, and the changing light transforms the bay throughout the day. These photographs capture my personal impressions of Sarandë—a place where the warmth of the Mediterranean, the depth of history, and the beauty of the Albanian Riviera come together along one remarkable stretch of coastline.
