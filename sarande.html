Sarandë, Albania

Albania · Albanian Riviera · Ionian Coast

Sarandë is one of Albania’s most captivating coastal towns, stretching along the turquoise waters of the Ionian Sea. Its history reaches back to antiquity, while its waterfront, palm-lined promenade, and lively streets give it the character of a modern Mediterranean destination. Beyond its beaches, Sarandë offers access to the cultural and natural treasures of southern Albania, including ancient sites and beautiful coastal landscapes. The sea shapes both the scenery and everyday life in Sarandë. Fishing boats share the horizon with ferries, cafés line the waterfront, and changing light transforms the bay throughout the day. These photographs capture my personal impressions of Sarandë, where Mediterranean warmth, history, and the beauty of the Albanian Riviera come together.
