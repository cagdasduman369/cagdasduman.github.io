Nikola Tesla Museum

Belgrade, Serbia

Located in the heart of Belgrade, the Nikola Tesla Museum offers a fascinating glimpse into the life and work of one of the most influential inventors in modern history. Dedicated to the Serbian-American scientist Nikola Tesla, the museum preserves his personal belongings, original documents, photographs and scientific legacy. What makes the visit particularly memorable is the opportunity to see demonstrations of some of his pioneering inventions and gain a closer understanding of the ideas that helped shape the modern world. It is a place where science, history and curiosity come together, bringing Tesla's remarkable imagination closer to visitors.
