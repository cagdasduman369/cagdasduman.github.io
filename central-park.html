Central Park

New York City · United States

Central Park is one of the most famous urban parks in the world, located in the heart of Manhattan. Opened in 1858, it offers a peaceful escape from the busy streets of New York City, with beautiful landscapes, walking paths, lakes, bridges and open green spaces. Visitors can enjoy scenic spots such as Bethesda Terrace, Bow Bridge and the Jacqueline Kennedy Onassis Reservoir. The park is a beloved destination for both locals and visitors, offering a wonderful opportunity to experience nature and relax in the middle of one of the world's busiest cities.
