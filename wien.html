Vienna, Austria

Austria · Vienna

Vienna, the capital of Austria, is a city where imperial history, remarkable architecture, and cultural traditions come together. Once the center of the Habsburg Empire, the city still reflects its royal past through magnificent palaces, elegant boulevards, historic squares, and beautifully preserved buildings. Known worldwide for its classical music heritage, museums, and traditional cafés, Vienna offers a rich cultural experience at every turn. Beyond its grand architecture and lively streets, the city also features peaceful green spaces and beautifully designed gardens that provide a different perspective on urban life. This collection captures my personal travel memories of Vienna, from its distinctive city views to the historic gardens of Burggarten and Stadtpark.
