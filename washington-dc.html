Washington, DC

United States

Washington, DC, the capital of the United States, is a city where American history, politics, architecture, and culture come together. As the seat of the federal government, it is home to institutions that have shaped the country's political life and democratic traditions. At the same time, the city is much more than its role as the nation's capital. Its iconic monuments, historic neighborhoods, world-renowned museums, religious landmarks, and cultural institutions reveal a rich and diverse urban landscape. From the National Mall and the U.S. Capitol to its museums, memorials, historic buildings, and quieter corners, Washington offers many different perspectives on American history and society. Walking through the city means encountering important national symbols alongside places that reflect everyday life, artistic expression, faith, and cultural diversity. This collection brings together my personal photographs and memories from Washington, DC, capturing its landmarks, architecture, museums, and the many places that make the city distinctive.
