Mrkonjić Grad, Bosnia and Herzegovina

Bosnia and Herzegovina · Republika Srpska · Western Bosnia

Located in the mountainous landscapes of western Bosnia and Herzegovina, Mrkonjić Grad is a place where nature, local traditions, and the quiet rhythm of small-town life come together. Surrounded by forests, hills, and clear rivers, the wider region offers a remarkable variety of natural scenery and opportunities to experience the countryside. Among its distinctive destinations is the village of Pecka, known for its impressive limestone landscapes and its growing role as a center for outdoor activities, environmental awareness, and community-based initiatives.

My time in this region offered an opportunity to discover places and experiences beyond the usual travel routes. From the climbing landscapes around Pecka and the community workshops at Pecka Visitor Center to the natural beauty of the Sana River springs, each location revealed a different aspect of the area's character. This collection brings together my personal photographs and memories of Mrkonjić Grad and its surroundings, capturing the landscapes, activities, and local experiences that made this visit particularly memorable.
