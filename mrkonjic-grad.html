Mrkonjić Grad, Bosnia and Herzegovina

Bosnia and Herzegovina · Republika Srpska · Western Bosnia

Located in the mountainous landscapes of western Bosnia and Herzegovina, Mrkonjić Grad is surrounded by forests, hills, and clear rivers. The wider region offers beautiful natural scenery and opportunities to experience the countryside. Nearby Pecka is particularly known for its limestone landscapes, outdoor activities, and community initiatives. My visit offered an opportunity to discover places beyond the usual travel routes. From the climbing area and community workshops at Pecka Visitor Center to the natural beauty of the Sana River springs, each location revealed a different aspect of the region. This collection brings together my personal photographs and memories of these experiences. The surrounding countryside also offers a closer look at the region's natural environment and local community life. The landscapes around Pecka, its outdoor recreation areas, and the clear waters of the Sana River reveal the quieter side of Bosnia and Herzegovina. Exploring these places provided an opportunity to appreciate the area's scenery, local initiatives, and distinctive atmosphere. These photographs preserve my personal impressions and memories of my time in the Mrkonjić Grad region.
