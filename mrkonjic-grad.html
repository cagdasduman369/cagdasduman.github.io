Mrkonjić Grad, Bosnia and Herzegovina

Bosnia and Herzegovina · Republika Srpska · Western Bosnia

Located in the mountainous landscapes of western Bosnia and Herzegovina, Mrkonjić Grad is surrounded by forests, hills, and clear rivers. The wider region offers beautiful natural scenery and opportunities to experience the countryside. Nearby Pecka is particularly known for its limestone landscapes, outdoor activities, and community initiatives. My visit offered an opportunity to discover places beyond the usual travel routes. From the climbing area and community workshops at Pecka Visitor Center to the natural beauty of the Sana River springs, each location revealed a different aspect of the region. This collection brings together my personal photographs and memories of these experiences.
