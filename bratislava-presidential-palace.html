Presidential Palace

Slovakia · Bratislava

Set in the heart of Bratislava, the Presidential Palace, also known as Grassalkovich Palace, is one of the city's most elegant architectural landmarks. Built in the 18th century, this graceful Baroque palace reflects the grandeur of an era when Bratislava was an important center of aristocratic life. Today, it serves as the official residence of the President of Slovakia, giving the historic building a special place in the country's political and cultural landscape. Its beautiful façade and carefully maintained gardens offer a peaceful contrast to the surrounding streets of the capital. The palace is a reminder of Bratislava's rich past and its continuing role as the center of Slovak public life.
