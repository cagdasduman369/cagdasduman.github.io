United States Capitol

Washington, DC · United States

The United States Capitol is one of the most recognizable landmarks in Washington, DC, and a central symbol of the country's political history. Located on Capitol Hill, it serves as the meeting place of the United States Congress, where the Senate and House of Representatives carry out their legislative responsibilities. Construction began in 1793, and the building has undergone several expansions and renovations throughout American history. Its distinctive neoclassical architecture, impressive dome, and carefully designed interior spaces reflect the nation's political traditions and institutional heritage. The surrounding Capitol grounds, with their open lawns, monuments, and historic buildings, form an important part of the city's civic landscape. Visiting the Capitol offers an opportunity to appreciate both its architectural character and its role in the development of American democracy.
