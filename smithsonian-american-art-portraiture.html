Smithsonian American Art Museum and National Portrait Gallery

Washington, DC · United States

Located in the heart of Washington, DC, the Smithsonian American Art Museum and the National Portrait Gallery share the historic Old Patent Office Building, bringing together two important collections under one roof. The American Art Museum presents works that reflect the country's artistic development, from early colonial times to contemporary creations. The National Portrait Gallery, meanwhile, features portraits of presidents, political leaders, artists, activists, and other influential figures who have shaped American history and culture.

Together, the collections offer visitors an opportunity to explore the nation's identity through art, history, and personal stories. The building itself is also remarkable, with impressive architecture and beautifully designed interior spaces. Walking through its galleries provides a different perspective on the people, events, and ideas that have influenced the United States.
