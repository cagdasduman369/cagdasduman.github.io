Smithsonian American Art Museum and National Portrait Gallery

Washington, DC · United States

Located in the heart of Washington, DC, the Smithsonian American Art Museum and the National Portrait Gallery share a historic building that brings together American art and portraits of influential figures. The collections offer a fascinating look at the country's artistic heritage, history, and cultural identity. These photographs capture moments from my visit to this remarkable museum complex.
