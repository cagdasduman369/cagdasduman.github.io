Stari Grad

Croatia · Island of Hvar · Adriatic Coast
Stari Grad, located on the northern coast of Hvar Island, is one of the oldest towns in Croatia and a place where history and Mediterranean life come together. Founded by ancient Greek settlers in the 4th century BC, the town has preserved much of its historic character through its narrow stone streets, traditional architecture, and peaceful harbour. Beyond its historic centre, Stari Grad offers a rich cultural atmosphere and beautiful coastal scenery. From the Church of St. Stephen to local celebrations and everyday views of the town, every corner reveals a different aspect of its identity. These photographs capture my personal travel memories and impressions of Stari Grad, preserving moments from this historic and charming Adriatic destination.
