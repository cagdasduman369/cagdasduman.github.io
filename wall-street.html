Wall Street

New York City · United States

Wall Street is one of the most influential financial districts in the world and a historic symbol of global capitalism. Located in Lower Manhattan, this famous street is home to the New York Stock Exchange and surrounded by some of the most recognizable financial institutions in the United States. Beyond its importance in international finance, Wall Street reflects the remarkable history and architectural character of New York City. Walking through its streets offers a glimpse into the economic power, history, and dynamic urban life that have helped shape the modern world. These photographs capture my personal memories of exploring this iconic part of Manhattan.
