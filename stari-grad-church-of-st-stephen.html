Church of St. Stephen

Stari Grad · Hvar Island · Croatia

Located in the historic heart of Stari Grad, the Church of St. Stephen is one of the town's most recognizable landmarks. Surrounded by narrow stone streets and traditional Dalmatian architecture, the church reflects the town's long history and its enduring Mediterranean character. Its setting offers a glimpse into the religious and cultural heritage that has shaped this historic settlement over many centuries. The surrounding streets and quiet atmosphere make this area a memorable place to explore on foot. These photographs capture my visit to this peaceful historic corner of Hvar Island and preserve personal memories of its architecture and surroundings.
