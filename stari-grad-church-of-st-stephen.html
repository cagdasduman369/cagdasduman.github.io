Church of St. Stephen

Stari Grad · Hvar Island · Croatia

Located in the historic heart of Stari Grad, the Church of St. Stephen is one of the town's most recognizable landmarks. Surrounded by narrow stone streets and traditional Dalmatian architecture, the church reflects the town's long history and its enduring Mediterranean character. These photographs capture my visit to this peaceful historic corner of Hvar Island.
