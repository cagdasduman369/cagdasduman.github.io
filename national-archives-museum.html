National Archives Museum

Washington, DC · United States

The National Archives Museum in Washington, DC, is one of the most important institutions preserving the documentary history of the United States. Located along the National Mall, it safeguards millions of records that trace the country's political, social, and cultural development. Its most famous treasures include the Declaration of Independence, the United States Constitution, and the Bill of Rights, collectively known as the Charters of Freedom. These historic documents provide insight into the principles, debates, and ideas that shaped the foundation of the American republic.

Beyond these central exhibits, the museum presents historical records, photographs, maps, and personal accounts that reveal different chapters of the nation's past. Its exhibitions also explore how government records are created, preserved, and used to understand history. A visit offers an opportunity to reflect on the importance of historical documents, democratic institutions, and public access to information.
