Trg Kralja Tomislava City Park

Zagreb · Croatia
Trg Kralja Tomislava is one of Zagreb's most recognizable public squares, located in the heart of the city's historic Lower Town. Named after King Tomislav, the first Croatian king, the square is framed by elegant architecture and features a spacious green park at its centre. Its flowerbeds, walking paths and open lawns create a welcoming atmosphere for both locals and visitors. With the impressive Art Pavilion standing at one end, the square offers a beautiful combination of urban heritage and green space.
