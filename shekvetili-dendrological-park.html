Shekvetili Dendrological Park

Shekvetili · Guria · Georgia

Located near Georgia's Black Sea coast, Shekvetili Dendrological Park is a remarkable green space where botanical diversity and carefully designed landscapes create an unusual setting for visitors. The park brings together trees and plants from different parts of the world, with open paths leading through spacious lawns, landscaped gardens, and distinctive natural surroundings. One of its most memorable features is the opportunity to see impressive trees and vegetation in a peaceful environment far removed from the busy coastal resorts. Walking through the grounds offers a chance to slow down, enjoy the scenery, and appreciate the variety of plant life. The combination of nature, space, and thoughtful landscaping makes the park a worthwhile stop while exploring the Shekvetili area.
