Tirana, Albania

Tirana, the vibrant capital of Albania, is a city where layers of history meet the energy of modern urban life. Its streets reveal a fascinating mix of Ottoman influences, Italian-inspired architecture, communist-era landmarks, and contemporary buildings, while colorful facades, lively squares, and cafés give the city its distinctive character. From the historic center to its expanding neighborhoods, Tirana reflects Albania's transformation and changing identity. Walking through the city offers countless opportunities to discover its architecture, everyday atmosphere, and unexpected urban perspectives. This collection captures my personal impressions of Tirana and the many details that make the city memorable.
