Himarë, Albania

Albania · Adriatic Coast

Himarë is a charming coastal town on the Albanian Riviera, known for its crystal-clear waters, beautiful beaches, and relaxed Mediterranean atmosphere. Surrounded by mountains and the Ionian Sea, it offers a wonderful blend of natural beauty, traditional Albanian culture, and picturesque seaside life.
