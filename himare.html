Himarë, Albania

Albania · Albanian Riviera · Ionian Coast

Himarë is a coastal town on the Albanian Riviera where the rugged mountains of southern Albania descend toward the deep blue waters of the Ionian Sea. Known for its crystal-clear beaches, traditional stone houses, and traces of a long and layered history, Himarë offers a quieter, more intimate perspective on Albania’s Mediterranean coastline. Its cultural heritage, shaped by centuries of local traditions and connections across the region, gives the town a character that extends far beyond its beautiful seafront.

What makes Himarë special is the balance between its natural beauty and the unhurried rhythm of everyday life. Narrow streets wind between old houses, the coastline opens onto secluded coves, and the surrounding hills provide a striking backdrop to the sea. These photographs capture my personal impressions of Himarë—a place where mountain landscapes, coastal traditions, and the changing colors of the Ionian Sea come together to create a memorable corner of the Albanian Riviera.
