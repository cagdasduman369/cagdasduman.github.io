BUNK'ART 2, Tirana

Albania · Tirana

BUNK'ART 2 is a museum in the heart of Tirana, housed in an underground bunker built during Albania's communist dictatorship. Dedicated to the history of the Ministry of Internal Affairs and the country's former security apparatus, its exhibitions explore surveillance, political persecution, interrogations, and the restrictions imposed on everyday life under the regime. Through historical documents, photographs, personal accounts, and preserved spaces, the museum offers visitors a deeper understanding of how state power and the secret police shaped Albanian society for decades. Its central location and thought-provoking exhibitions make it an important place to reflect on Albania's difficult past and the lasting impact of political repression. These photographs capture my personal impressions of this significant historical site in Tirana.
