National Museum of American History

Washington, DC · United States

The National Museum of American History, part of the Smithsonian Institution, preserves and presents the stories of the people, events, and ideas that have shaped the United States. Located on the National Mall in Washington, DC, the museum brings together an extensive collection of historical objects, cultural artifacts, documents, and interactive exhibitions. Its displays explore many aspects of American life, from political history and scientific innovation to music, entertainment, transportation, and everyday culture. Among its well-known objects are the original Star-Spangled Banner, important presidential artifacts, and items connected to major moments in the nation's development.

Visitors can discover how social changes, technological achievements, and individual experiences have influenced American society over time. The museum also examines the country's diverse cultural traditions and the different perspectives that contribute to its national story. Each exhibition offers a new way to understand the history and identity of the United States.
