Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina

Bosnia and Herzegovina · Balkans

Nestled in a valley surrounded by the dramatic peaks of the Dinaric Alps, Sarajevo is a city where history, cultures and traditions converge. Often described as a meeting point between East and West, its streets reveal layers of Ottoman, Austro-Hungarian and contemporary European heritage. The historic Baščaršija district, with its traditional shops, cobbled streets and distinctive architecture, reflects the city's rich Ottoman legacy, while elegant buildings from the Austro-Hungarian period tell another chapter of its past. The Miljacka River flows through the heart of the city, connecting historic neighbourhoods and iconic bridges. Sarajevo's remarkable cultural diversity, vibrant everyday life and profound historical character make it one of the most distinctive and fascinating cities in the Balkans.
