National Flag Museum

Baku · Azerbaijan

Located on Baku's Caspian Sea waterfront, the National Flag Museum forms part of the National Flag Square complex, an important landmark dedicated to the country's national symbols. The museum explores the history and development of Azerbaijan's flag, presenting its meaning across different historical periods. Its collection includes historical flags, official documents, photographs, and various objects that illustrate the evolution of national symbols. Through these exhibits, visitors can gain a broader understanding of Azerbaijan's history, cultural heritage, and the role of the national flag in shaping the country's identity.

During my visit, I explored the museum's exhibition spaces and observed the different ways in which the country's history and national symbols are presented. The displays, historical materials, and architectural details offered several interesting photographic subjects. I was particularly interested in the visual presentation of the exhibits and the historical stories behind them. This collection brings together my personal photographs and memories from the National Flag Museum, documenting my visit to this distinctive cultural landmark in Baku.
