National Flag Museum

Baku · Azerbaijan

Located along Baku's Caspian Sea waterfront, the National Flag Museum is part of the National Flag Square complex, one of the city's most recognisable landmarks. The museum celebrates Azerbaijan's national symbols and presents the history, meaning and development of the country's flag. Inside, visitors can discover historical flags, official documents, photographs and other exhibits that illustrate different periods of Azerbaijani history. The collection offers an insight into the country's national identity and the symbolism associated with its flag. During my visit, I explored the museum and photographed some of its displays and architectural details. This collection brings together my personal photographs and memories from the National Flag Museum in Baku.
