Burggarten

Austria · Vienna · Burggarten

Burggarten is one of Vienna's most charming historic gardens, located near the Hofburg Imperial Palace. Originally created as a private garden for the Habsburg imperial family, it later became a public green space offering a peaceful escape in the heart of the city. Its lawns, mature trees, flower beds, and winding paths create a pleasant contrast to Vienna's busy streets. The garden is also home to the famous Mozart Monument and the beautiful Palmenhaus, a striking glass structure that adds to its distinctive character. Today, Burggarten remains a lovely place to relax and appreciate a quieter side of the Austrian capital. This collection brings together my personal photographs and memories of the garden's natural beauty and historic atmosphere.
