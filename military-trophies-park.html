Military Trophies Park

Baku · Azerbaijan

Military Trophies Park is an outdoor exhibition space in Baku, featuring military equipment and vehicles captured during the 2020 Nagorno-Karabakh war. Situated near the Caspian Sea, the park displays tanks, armored vehicles, and other military equipment across its open grounds. The exhibits are presented as part of a broader narrative about Azerbaijan's recent history and the events surrounding the conflict. Through its arrangement and selection of objects, the site offers an insight into how historical events and national experiences are represented in public spaces.

Walking through the park provides an opportunity to observe the scale and variety of the exhibits, as well as the way the exhibition has been designed within its outdoor setting. During my visit, I photographed the military displays, the surrounding landscape, and different details throughout the grounds. These images document both the physical features of the park and the atmosphere of the site, preserving my personal impressions of this distinctive location in Baku. This collection brings together my photographs and memories from the visit.
