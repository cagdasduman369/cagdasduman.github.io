Military Trophies Park

Baku · Azerbaijan

Military Trophies Park is an outdoor exhibition area in Baku displaying military equipment and vehicles captured during the 2020 Nagorno-Karabakh war. Located near the Caspian Sea, the park presents a collection of tanks, military vehicles and other equipment arranged throughout its open grounds. The site offers a particular perspective on how the conflict is represented in Azerbaijan's public spaces. Its displays combine military objects with a carefully arranged exhibition environment, attracting visitors interested in the country's recent history and the region's political developments.

During my visit, I photographed the exhibition and its surroundings, documenting the displays and the atmosphere of the site. This collection brings together my personal photographs and memories from Military Trophies Park in Baku.
