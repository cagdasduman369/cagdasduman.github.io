Lezhë, Albania

Albania · Northwestern Albania

Lezhë is a historic town in northwestern Albania where the stories of the country's past meet the quiet beauty of its surrounding landscapes. With roots reaching back to the ancient city of Lissus, Lezhë has occupied an important position at the crossroads of Mediterranean and Balkan history. Its most enduring historical association is with Gjergj Kastrioti Skanderbeg, Albania's celebrated national hero, whose alliance of Albanian nobles was established here in 1444. The town's historic landmarks offer a glimpse into the events and people that helped shape Albania's national identity.

Beyond its historical significance, Lezhë reveals a quieter side of Albania, with its castle overlooking the town, nearby rivers and wetlands, and the Adriatic coastline within easy reach. The surrounding region brings together cultural heritage, open landscapes, and the rhythms of everyday local life. These photographs capture my personal impressions of Lezhë and its surroundings—a place where history remains part of the landscape and where even a short visit can open a window onto Albania's rich and complex past.
