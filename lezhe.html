Lezhë, Albania

Albania · Northwestern Albania

Lezhë is a historic town in northwestern Albania, known for its ancient roots and important place in Balkan history. Its strongest historical connection is with Gjergj Kastrioti Skanderbeg, Albania's national hero, whose alliance of Albanian nobles was established here in 1444. The town's historic landmarks reflect an important chapter in Albania's national identity.

Beyond its history, Lezhë offers castle views, nearby rivers and wetlands, and easy access to the Adriatic coast. The surrounding region combines cultural heritage, open landscapes, and everyday local life. These photographs capture my personal impressions of Lezhë and its surroundings.
