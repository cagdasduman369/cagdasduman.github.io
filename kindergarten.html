Craiova – Kindergarten

Romania · Craiova

My visit to a kindergarten in Craiova was one of the most heartwarming and enjoyable experiences of my time in Romania. The Romanian children were incredibly sweet, cheerful and full of energy, and their innocent smiles and playful personalities made the experience truly special. Spending time in their company gave me a lovely opportunity to connect with local people in a warm and meaningful way, beyond the usual experiences of sightseeing and exploring a new city. Their enthusiasm and kindness brought so much joy to the day, leaving me with wonderful memories that I will always cherish. It was a simple yet deeply rewarding experience that reminded me how small moments of human connection can make travelling so memorable.
