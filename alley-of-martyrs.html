Alley of Martyrs

Baku · Azerbaijan
The Alley of Martyrs is a memorial cemetery in Baku overlooking the Caspian Sea. It commemorates those who lost their lives during significant events in Azerbaijan's modern history. With its solemn atmosphere and panoramic views of the city and coastline, the site offers a place for remembrance and reflection.
