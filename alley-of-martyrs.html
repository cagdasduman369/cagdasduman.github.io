Alley of Martyrs

Baku · Azerbaijan
The Alley of Martyrs is one of the most significant memorial sites in Baku, overlooking the Caspian Sea and offering a striking view of the city. It is the resting place of those who lost their lives during the Black January events of 1990 and the First Nagorno-Karabakh War. Walking through the memorial grounds, visitors encounter rows of graves, monuments and eternal flames that reflect the country's painful past and collective memory. Beyond its historical significance, the site is also a place of quiet reflection, where the atmosphere of remembrance blends with the beautiful views of Baku and the Caspian coastline. Visiting the Alley of Martyrs offers a deeper perspective on Azerbaijan's modern history and the events that have shaped its national identity.
