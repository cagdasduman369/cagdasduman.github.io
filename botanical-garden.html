Batumi Botanical Garden

Batumi · Georgia

Located near the Black Sea coast, Batumi Botanical Garden is one of Georgia's most remarkable natural attractions. Established in the late nineteenth century, the garden covers a vast area overlooking the sea and features an impressive collection of plants from different parts of the world. Its winding pathways, lush greenery, and scenic viewpoints create a peaceful atmosphere for exploring nature. Walking through the garden offers beautiful views of the coastline and surrounding landscapes. These photographs capture my personal memories of Batumi Botanical Garden and its natural beauty. The garden's diverse landscapes make every part of the visit a little different. It is also a wonderful place to enjoy the contrast between rich vegetation and the open sea.
